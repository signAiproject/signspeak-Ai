HOW IT WORKS

From sign to understanding.

1

Capture

The camera receives the sign after you grant permission.

2

Track

Hand landmarks can be detected with browser-based computer vision.

3

Recognise

A trained ISL model must classify signs and movements.

4

Communicate

Supported predictions can be shown as English text and spoken aloud.

Important: This is a starter prototype. Hand tracking alone does not translate sign language. No trained ISL classifier is included yet, so live camera and uploaded files are not automatically translated. Full ISL translation requires validated ISL data and a model that accounts for movement and context.
